# AcuityMD

> AcuityMD is the leading commercial intelligence platform for medical technology (MedTech) companies. Built on the industry's most comprehensive healthcare data, AcuityMD helps medical device sales teams identify high-value targets, understand physician referral networks, manage territories and pipelines, accelerate contract performance, and drive commercial excellence—from growth-stage startups to top-10 MedTech enterprises.


## Platform

- [AcuityMD Platform Overview](https://www.acuitymd.com/platform): The full commercial intelligence platform for MedTech sales—targeting, pipeline, territory management, contracts, and AI-powered forecasting in one unified system.
- [MedTech AI Platform](https://www.acuitymd.com/platform/medtech-ai-platform): AcuityMD's AI-powered layer that surfaces intelligent recommendations, automates analysis, and helps sales teams act on data faster.
- [Care Journeys](https://www.acuitymd.com/platform/products/care-journeys): Map full patient care journeys and physician referral networks to find where your device fits in the care continuum and identify upstream influencers.
- [Contracts](https://www.acuitymd.com/platform/products/contracts): Manage GPO, IDN, and facility contracts to maximize compliance, accelerate sales cycles, and prevent revenue from being left on the table.
- [Markets](https://www.acuitymd.com/platform/products/markets): Understand market sizing, procedure volumes, and site-of-care shifts to prioritize the highest-value accounts and geographies.
- [Pipeline](https://www.acuitymd.com/platform/products/pipeline): AI-assisted pipeline management and revenue forecasting built on real-world procedure data—not manual CRM entry.
- [Targeting](https://www.acuitymd.com/platform/products/targeting): Identify and prioritize high-value physicians and facilities using claims data, procedure volumes, and referral patterns.
- [Territories](https://www.acuitymd.com/platform/products/territories): Design, analyze, and optimize sales territories using data-driven insights to ensure equitable coverage and maximum revenue potential.
- [Forecasting](https://www.acuitymd.com/platform/products/forecasting): Improve sales forecast accuracy with AI models trained on historical procedure data and market signals.
- [Integrations](https://www.acuitymd.com/platform/products/integrations): Connect AcuityMD with your existing CRM, ERP, and data systems to streamline commercial workflows and extend the platform across your MedTech tech stack.
